How Loyaltea works

Easy for customers, simple for staff, and quick to launch.

01

Create your offer

Choose points, prepaid bundles, subscriptions, or punch cards and customize the reward logic.

02

Add the pass to wallet

Customers join from a QR code or link and save the pass to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

03

Scan at checkout

Your team scans the pass and Loyaltea updates visits, points, balances, or rewards automatically.
